### RL-1 — Retained reports and scoped audit/privacy duties

Date: 2026-10-27 · Ruler: ct · Governs: REPORT-002, TEAM-003, PRIVACY-001, PRIVACY-002, PRIVACY-003

APPROVE WITH RIDERS; stays PARTIAL. Counts remain 61 done, 34 partial, 32 missing, 127 total. The owner-only interim default is an evidence-preservation floor, not completion. No acceptance point is removed or added; approved riders refine existing proof under its owning clauses.

The live native owner may preserve the exact reviewed report with an explicit reason and affirmative owner authorization, retaining its original JSON, CSV, source revisions, integrity digests and authorization through later corrections, restart and supported installation archive restore. The consent control records owner authorization; it establishes neither employee consent nor a statutory legal basis. Existing source-specific consent duties remain.

Interim operation remains existing-owner-only, with no new reader grants and no automatic expiry or erasure. Indefinite storage describes the current technical behavior; it is not a ruling that every copied personal fact may lawfully be kept forever. The owner can prepare, inspect, preserve and download an authorized audit packet now, and remains responsible for separately reviewed external disclosures and employee requests. Owner credentials must not be shared.

Owner-only administration is too narrow for the contracted operations buyer. TEAM-001 and ACCESS-003 must provide a separately granted, read-only audit permission for a named authenticated person, bounded by business, sites, employees, jurisdictions, report classes and date range, with an explicit purpose and access end date. Report access and download require current authority on every request and immediate revocation. Wage and other sensitive fields require their separate permission and necessary-purpose review. An audit grant confers no staffing, approval, payroll, integration or privacy-administration authority and cannot exceed the grantor’s content authority. Private leave purposes, private notes and unrelated personal data remain excluded from the ordinary audit projection.

PRIVACY-001 governs retention, holds and employee-rights disposition for report sources and retained copies. Initial maintained retention presets must distinguish federal payroll records at three years, federal wage-computation records at two years, Chicago fair-workweek records at three years, Chicago paid-leave records at five years, and applicable California hours, wage and sick-leave records at three years, while preserving longer employment-duration, jurisdictional and other applicable duties. Each preset records its source, effective date, applicability and retention anchor. The longest applicable obligation controls; unknown applicability remains explicitly unresolved and cannot authorize deletion. A retained report cannot shorten its sources’ duties.

Every applicable hold independently blocks disposition of its covered sources and copies. Releasing one hold does not release another or initiate deletion. Employee access, export and correction requests require identity and authority review, employee-scoped disclosure and an attributable response; eligible erasure requires a separate reviewed action that rechecks current duties and every hold, removes covered local derivatives and saved results, and advances restore fences. Ordinary employee exports retain their existing visibility rules; statutory requests requiring additional material need separately reviewed fulfillment rather than a blanket refusal based on that visibility setting. Disconnected archives, downloads and provider copies require explicit external handling. Unsupported disposition remains open and cannot be reported as fulfilled. No legal compliance or complete provider erasure is established by this ruling.

Authority proof belongs to TEAM-001 and ACCESS-003; retention, holds and disposition proof belongs to PRIVACY-001. REPORT-002 references these duties.
