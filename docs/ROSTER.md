# Roster and leave

Build a Monday-starting week from recorded availability and approved leave. A draft is preparation; publishing is a separate human decision.

## Record availability and leave

Staff open **Roster**, then **My availability and leave**. Use **Add availability** to save an available start and end, or **Request leave** to enter the dates and a reason. Managers can also use **Record staff availability**.

Availability must cover the whole shift. Adjacent availability windows can cover it together. In **Leave**, a manager or the owner selects **Approve leave** or **Reject leave** for another person's request. Pending leave is not an approved absence; decide requests before publishing.

## Prepare the week

1. Check the venue timezone shown in the app. The owner sets it in **People** before the first publication.
2. In **Prepare a week**, choose **Week starting Monday**.
3. Add the person, start, end, unpaid break minutes and optional label for each shift. Use **Add another shift** as needed.
4. Select **Prepare draft** and inspect the week, shifts and any **Draft conflicts**.

Enter dates and times in the venue's local time. A shift must start within the selected venue week. Missing or ambiguous wall times during a daylight-saving change are refused; choose an unambiguous boundary.

Preparing the same week again replaces its current draft. It does not publish it or replace the published week yet.

## Resolve conflicts and publish

Publication checks that every person is active, their availability covers the shift, they have no overlapping approved leave and their shifts do not overlap. Conflicting drafts can be reviewed, but cannot be published.

Correct the source of a conflict, prepare the week again if its shifts change, and review the current revision. In **Weeks**, select **Publish this revision**. If somebody has changed the draft since you loaded it, reload and review the new revision before publishing.

Publishing a replacement week supersedes the earlier published roster and its shifts. Staff see their own current published shifts.

## Confirm your shifts

Staff open **Roster**, review their own published shifts, then select **Confirm shift** on each one. The card records confirmation. A draft does not ask staff to confirm; publish first.
