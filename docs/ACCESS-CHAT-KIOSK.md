# Access levels, shared clocks and operational conversations

These capabilities are partial; CONTRACT.md and ACCEPTANCE.md list the remaining work and policy defaults awaiting review.

Open **Access** in the app to create a cafe-specific level, select fixed catalog permissions, assign it to an employee, inspect permission history or revoke the assignment. Permissions are additive across that cafe's active levels. A custom assignment switches that employee away from broad legacy manager access, even after its revocation; an authorized administrator must deliberately assign the required authority again. Only the owner may initially switch a native manager into custom mode. Managers administer levels and grants only at their own cafe for their own cafe’s people, including levels with no permissions. Supported custom commands currently cover scoped draft preparation/publication, swap/leave/time approval, punch editing and payroll file operations. Unsupported administrative commands and report reads deny. Existing native manager defaults remain until a custom assignment is made. Custom commands currently require the employee's home cafe; business/department and multi-site delegation remain open. Payroll operations also require wage permission. Preparation keys are separate and never inherit a level.

The Access page also holds your personal six-digit kiosk PIN and English/Spanish kiosk preference. Only the owner enrolls and revokes a device at an existing cafe. A locked device must be revoked and enrolled again. Five failed verifications lock the device; per-person failures also limit PIN attempts across devices for fifteen minutes. No employee list is shown by the kiosk. Opening `/app/kiosk` ends this browser’s native app session before enabling employee verification, so an enrollment/admin login does not carry over to employees. Enrollment forms redirect there. Verify your email and PIN, capture your own time and timed meal, then sign out before handing the tablet over. Employee sessions last five minutes and cannot open the normal application. A device cannot capture at another cafe. Job changes use the existing qualification, network and segmented-time commands.

Photo collection needs explicit employer approval and consent from the employee for each still. The owner sets site-wide requirements in **People → Punch photo custody**; an enrolled device can also require a still for every capture. Site policy never replaces employer consent at enrollment: a device enrolled without that consent must be revoked and re-enrolled with it before capturing under a required-photo policy. The client opens its camera only at a consented required punch and stops every track immediately after taking the still. The site-only policy can require break photos too; a stronger each-capture device enrollment keeps that requirement. No face matching or template is used.

An unavailable camera or declined consent keeps the punch with a named exception that bulk approval skips. Only the employee and owner can download its photo at `/api/lanyard/kiosk-photos/PHOTO_ID`; other approvers receive neither image IDs nor bytes. The owner places or releases a reasoned hold and explicitly erases photos past the site's elapsed-day retention period. Former-employee erasure also requires deactivation. Holds prevent erasure; nothing deletes automatically. Erasure retains punches, exceptions and erasure receipts and fences older archives when restoring with the later journal. Protect and retire old offline archives yourself.

Device tokens, employee-session tokens, PIN digests and clock-browser credentials stay out of archives. Restoring requires fresh enrollment and PIN setup. Product notices, receipts and eligible photo bytes travel in the private installation backup. Physical phone/tablet camera acceptance and wider manager photo readers remain unproved; the CLOCK-005 custody/retention defaults await policy confirmation.

The kiosk saves pending captures encrypted in this browser using the existing clock vault. Retry sends them in order using their original identities. A server refusal retains the original for review. The committed kioskOffline journey proves encrypted page/Worker restart, ordered clock/meal replay, no duplication, revoked-device count-only owner recovery before employee retry and reasoned resolution without automatic application. The same journey now proves photo-required clock and meal stills across encrypted page/Worker restart and revoked-device recovery without images in the manager report. Offline job-transfer variants remain unproved, so full CLOCK-002 inheritance is still partial. Sign-out warns before deleting any pending originals. Clearing browser storage also removes them. No device-reported capture becomes approved time automatically.

Open **Chat** for operational direct/group/site threads, manager announcements and handovers pinned to the original published shift revision. Direct/group messages require explicit active members at that cafe. Site threads use current cafe membership. A shift handover follows the current assignee and native cafe managers; it preserves the original shift and revision as context. Transfer removes old-cafe access immediately, including attachment downloads, and deactivation refuses the native session. Owners have no implicit direct-message membership. Announcements are delivered in Lanyard only; no email, push or SMS delivery is claimed.

Messages are plain text, with one optional attachment up to 100 KB, downloaded through the thread's current authorization. Search covers the latest 100 matching permitted messages. Acknowledgment is per message and employee, with a retained timestamp. Keep HR/payroll facts in their private workflows: the application never automatically copies those facts into chat, but it cannot certify user-written prose. A swap request in chat changes no shift. Use Schedule's proposal, employee consent and exact manager approval. Time approval remains on Time. Messaging retention and erasure policy awaits review.
