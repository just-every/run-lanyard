# Schedule and time off

Build a Monday-starting week of shifts from recorded availability and approved time off. A draft is preparation; publishing the schedule is a separate human decision.

## Record availability and time off

Staff open **Schedule**, then **My availability and leave**. Use **Add availability** to save when they can start and finish, or **Request leave** to enter the dates and a reason for time off. Managers can also use **Record staff availability**.

Availability must cover the whole shift. Back-to-back availability windows can cover it together. In **Leave**, a manager or the owner selects **Approve leave** or **Reject leave** for another person's time-off request. A pending request is not approved time off; decide requests before publishing.

## Prepare the week

1. Check the venue time zone shown in the app. The owner sets it in **People** before the first schedule is published.
2. In **Prepare a week**, choose **Week starting Monday**, for example the week of Oct 19, 2026.
3. Add the person, start, end, unpaid break minutes and optional label for each shift. Use **Add another shift** as needed.
4. Select **Prepare draft** and check the week, its shifts and any **Draft conflicts**.

Enter dates and times in the venue's local time. A shift must start within the selected week. Times that are skipped or repeated when daylight saving time starts or ends are refused; choose a time that happens once.

Preparing the same week again replaces its current draft. It does not publish it or replace the published week yet.

## Resolve conflicts and publish

Publishing checks that every person is active, their availability covers the shift, they have no overlapping approved time off and their shifts do not overlap. You can review a draft with conflicts, but you cannot publish it.

Fix what causes a conflict, prepare the week again if its shifts change, and review the current revision. In **Weeks**, select **Publish this revision**. If somebody has changed the draft since you loaded it, reload and review the new revision before publishing.

Publishing a replacement week supersedes the earlier published schedule and its shifts. Staff see their own current published shifts.

## Confirm your shifts

Staff open **Schedule**, review their own published shifts, then select **Confirm shift** on each one. The shift records the confirmation. A draft does not ask staff to confirm; publish first.
