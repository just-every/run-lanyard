# Product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Lanyard is a complete US hourly-workforce product: build and fill schedules, handle swaps and call-outs, capture actual work, control labor cost and overtime, allocate tips, and reconcile approved hours with payroll. A buyer of Deputy, When I Work, Homebase or 7shifts can move this whole operational job to Lanyard; Connecteam and Legion set additional mobile and optimization depth. The first buyer runs a 10–100 person US service, retail or hospitality business. Currency and budgets are USD; sites use US time zones, daylight saving and employer-reviewed federal/state/local policy.

This contract is the product destination, not a claim that every target ships today. The Status section records the code and test evidence. Existing guarantees remain part of the full job; the old Australian single-venue framing and exclusions of swaps, tips, pay-cost calculation, multi-site work and live provider submission no longer define the target. This round changes documentation only. Provider access, agreements, app distribution and policy engineering remain delivery work.

## Existing guarantees

- **ACCESS-001 — Native sessions and server-side authority.** Owners grant access; managers make staffing and time decisions; staff see their own private work. Every private page, state read, mutation and payroll download checks the live native session, role and ownership on the server. Browser mutations require the configured origin. Deactivation revokes existing staff access immediately; a manager cannot approve their own time or leave. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as owner, manager and two staff through native invitation/confirmation routes; use the app and HTTP commands, attempt cross-person reads, self-approval, signed-out and cross-origin mutations, then deactivate a signed-in employee. Unauthorized attempts fail without writes.

- **PEOPLE-001 — Staff lifecycle and payroll identities.** The owner adds, edits, activates, invites and deactivates employees with unique email and payroll identifiers and an explicit staff or manager role. Stale revisions cannot overwrite newer people records. Invitation and activation remain separate actions; removing access retains employment history. **Acceptance evidence:** In People, create and invite staff, reject duplicate email/payroll IDs, edit the exact revision and deactivate a signed-in person. Their prior shifts and hours remain while their private requests are denied.

- **MOVE-001 — Reviewed Staff CSV move-in.** The owner previews Staff CSV v1 without writes and applies only the exact reviewed fingerprint. Imported people start inactive and uninvited. Original inputs, mapping provenance, results and an import journal remain inspectable; undo removes untouched imports and preserves later native edits and dependent work. This documented format is not a verified vendor adapter. **Acceptance evidence:** Paste Staff CSV in Move in, preview and inspect it, reject changed bytes at apply, apply the reviewed input, read its originals/journal, edit one imported person and undo. Only untouched rows disappear and nobody is invited automatically.

- **ROSTER-001 — Availability and explicit leave decisions.** Staff record availability and request dated time off; managers approve or reject the exact pending request. Pending requests do not silently become approved absence. Self-approval and approval that collides with an already published shift are refused; the manager resolves coverage explicitly. **Acceptance evidence:** Through staff and manager app/command doors record availability, request overlapping leave, reject self-approval, approve a valid request and attempt a published-shift conflict. Inspect the decision author and retained state.

- **ROSTER-002 — Conflict-checked revision publication.** Managers or delegated agents prepare a weekly roster, then a human manager publishes the exact revision. Publication rechecks active staff, availability, approved leave and overlapping assignments atomically. Republishing supersedes the previous week revision without deleting its work history; stale drafts fail. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare an invalid week through the roster API and see named conflicts and refused publication. Prepare a valid week, attempt stale approval, publish as a manager and inspect superseded records and activity. A changed input requires fresh review.

- **ROSTER-003 — Personal published schedule confirmation.** Employees see their own current published shifts and confirm the exact shift revision. A confirmation records who and when, and cannot acknowledge a superseded shift or somebody else's assignment. Confirmation indicates the employee's response, not completed work. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish a roster, open it as each employee and confirm a personal shift. Attempt a foreign or stale confirmation; inspect the visible published assignment and confirmation separately from clock records.

- **TIME-001 — Identity-bound phone capture and replay.** A signed-in employee clocks in and out with original capture time, receipt time and a stable command identity. Retrying the same body/person returns the same result without duplicate time; reusing its identity for a changed body or person fails. Only one open clock is allowed and captured unpaid break minutes reduce worked minutes. The present page retry facility requires the browser to remain open; durable offline capture is CLOCK-002. **Acceptance evidence:** Use staff clock commands to start and stop a shift, replay both events, and reuse an identity with another time/person. Read one closed clock and one submitted timesheet with correct net minutes; an employee cannot approve it.

- **TIME-002 — Exact time approval and linked corrections.** Managers review submitted hours, prepare reasoned missed time and approve the exact revision. A correction links to the latest approved source, retains the original and requires fresh approval; overlap and future-time checks apply. Employees never approve their own work and a pending correction prevents exporting its obsolete source. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare missed time, approve it through a manager session, correct its break or duration, approve the correction and inspect retained superseded history. Reject self/stale approval and overlapping work; attempt payroll export while a correction awaits decision.

- **PAYROLL-001 — Reviewed once-only payroll files and signed adjustments.** A manager reviews the exact approved-source CSV and explicitly exports that revision. Unique source claims prevent two batches claiming the same time revision. Downloads verify immutable bytes and hash and are manager-only; later corrections export the signed difference from previously claimed time. Payroll IDs must still match at export. Downloading twice does not create a new batch, but cannot prevent somebody uploading a file twice outside Lanyard. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare two competing batches through payroll commands, export one and reject the other. Download identical bytes twice through the authenticated CSV route, refuse staff access, then approve a negative correction and export only its delta while the old download stays unchanged.

- **PAYROLL-002 — Explicit manual provider acknowledgement.** After downloading a payroll file, a manager records the provider reference against the exact exported batch. Lanyard labels this as a person-recorded acknowledgement. It does not imply a live transfer, provider readback, payroll application or wage payment; those states belong to SYNC-001 and the named connectors. **Acceptance evidence:** Export hours, record a provider reference through payroll.acknowledge, reload the batch and inspect its reference, actor and time. A draft cannot be acknowledged and a stale batch revision is refused.

- **AGENT-001 — Preparation-only delegated authority.** The owner issues expiring revocable preparation keys. Agents read a reduced context and prepare roster, time and payroll drafts; only a native human manager publishes, approves or exports. Agents receive no payroll IDs, download files or staff contact details through their context. Server authority applies through both human and agent doors. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a key, read agent context and prepare time; attempt approval/export through both HTTP doors, approve as a human, revoke the key and immediately retry. Inspect redacted context and distinct agent preparation versus human decisions.

- **ARCHIVE-001 — Complete installation archive with authority exclusions.** Owners export and atomically restore supported people, original owner identity, rosters, time revisions, payroll files/source claims, events and import originals/journals into an empty compatible installation. Corrupt or populated imports fail. Sessions, sign-in links, agent keys, provider credentials and the development outbox do not travel; configuration is re-entered and the archived owner signs in again. This retained-owner policy deliberately replaces a generic Base restore that might establish a new owner. A destination or supplied later erasure journal fences older erased identities; disconnected old copies cannot know about later erasure. **Acceptance evidence:** Use public rer export/import commands on an installation with staff, published shifts, corrected/exported hours and import history. Round-trip records and files, reject damaged/populated imports, inspect excluded authority and sign in anew as the archived owner; supply a later erasure journal to refuse resurrection.

- **ERASE-001 — Unused identity erasure and restore fence.** An owner erases only an unused, uninvited, never-imported staff identity, replacing personal values and recording an erasure fence that rejects recreation/import by the erased identity. Employed, invited or imported people are deactivated and their legally relevant history retained; expanded retention and privacy controls are PRIVACY-001. **Acceptance evidence:** Create an unused person, erase via its exact revision, inspect the state for removed name/email, refuse native/CSV recreation and refuse an older archive with the later fence. Attempt erasing invited or imported staff and require retention with deactivation available.

- **AUDIT-001 — Durable decisions and transactional history.** Publication, time decisions, payroll export/acknowledgement, import actions, erasure and payroll downloads retain actor, role, subject and time. State mutation, payroll source claims, decision event and replay outcome commit together or fail together. Audit survives restart and supported archives; role-scoped history is inspectable without exposing employee-private data. **Acceptance evidence:** Perform the roster-to-payroll journey through public doors, inspect each actor/revision/event, restart and round-trip the archive. Cause a stale concurrent mutation and verify no orphan decision or source claim appears; staff cannot read management activity.

- **PUBLIC-001 — Public run site and installed Lobby.** The public run site presents the target product, opens /run for installation and builds owner guides at /docs from repository Markdown. The installed / remains the shared Lobby, reflecting the durable owner claim before separate first-run confirmation. Public marketing, installation and private operational state stay distinguishable; enabling deployment remains an explicit repository operation. **Acceptance evidence:** Build the run site, follow homepage to /run and /docs, then claim an installation through native setup and inspect the installed Lobby before and after first-run confirmation. Private product data remains session-protected.

- **BASE-001 — Maintained installation and operations commands.** The maintained command surface remains SEED-COMMAND-CONTRACT.md: pinned dependencies, setup, dev/preview, build/check/verify, owner-account deploy, export/import, diagnostics and explicit reset. Product-owned archive and erasure policy replaces only the stated Base data behavior; native auth, deployment identities and shared Lobby are retained. Build/deploy never run tests and development credentials cannot enter hosted output. **Acceptance evidence:** From a fresh clone use documented command help and structural check, launch and sign in, build and verify, exercise backup/restore, then perform a provider-authorized deployment and health readback. Reset requires explicit confirmation and never removes another installation.

## Workforce records and schedule building

- **TEAM-001 — Multi-location roles and delegated management.** Owners organize businesses, locations, departments and jobs; assign employees to multiple eligible sites; and scope schedulers, site managers, time approvers, payroll administrators and read-only analysts independently. Wage and tip data are restricted. Cross-location staff see permitted assignments while managers cannot act beyond their sites; owners can revoke grants immediately. **Acceptance evidence:** Create two locations with a shared employee and separate managers through organization settings. Use each role to schedule, approve, report and export; reject foreign-site actions and wage reads, then revoke a grant during an active session.

- **PEOPLE-002 — Jobs qualifications preferences and effective pay rates.** Managers maintain job skills, certifications with expiry, site eligibility, employee preferences, minimum/maximum hours, employment classification and effective-dated USD wage/job rates. Qualification expiry blocks unsuitable assignment and private wage access is restricted. Changes preserve the rate/policy basis used for past costs and payroll preparation. **Acceptance evidence:** Add a cross-trained employee with expiring certification, different job rates and preferences; schedule across the expiry/rate boundary, reject an unqualified shift and inspect historical cost snapshots after a rate change.

- **SCHEDULE-001 — Editable visual schedules and publication review.** Managers build day/week schedules with drag-and-drop, resize, copy and bulk assignment, seeing roles, sites, headcount, breaks, coverage gaps and live conflict/cost warnings. Drafts remain private until a reviewed publish/change diff. Undoing a draft edit cannot erase published history; mobile managers can perform the same core scheduling work. **Acceptance evidence:** Build a realistic service week in the schedule UI, move and bulk-edit shifts, inspect warnings and the employee-facing change diff, publish it and verify employee views. Repeat core edits at 390px and reject stale publication.

- **SCHEDULE-002 — Reusable templates recurring shifts and duplication.** Managers save schedules and role-based shifts as templates, duplicate selected days/weeks, and generate recurring assignments with explicit end dates and exceptions. Applying a template rechecks current leave, availability, qualifications, budgets and policies without auto-publishing or copying stale confirmations. **Acceptance evidence:** Save a week, duplicate it into a holiday week and apply a recurring pattern with an exception. Review conflicts for an employee now on leave, edit only one occurrence and publish after approval without altering previous weeks.

- **SCHEDULE-003 — Cross-site coverage and double-booking prevention.** One schedule spans multiple locations and time zones with location/role coverage requirements. Employee conflicts, travel time, minimum rest, overtime and hours budgets are calculated across all their sites. Authorized managers coordinate transfers without competing bookings; site-local times, overnight shifts and US daylight saving transitions remain explicit. **Acceptance evidence:** Build schedules for two US sites sharing staff, including an overnight and DST-boundary shift. Try overlapping and impossible-travel assignments, compare cross-site hours and publish a valid coverage plan through authorized managers.

- **SCHEDULE-004 — Shift notes tasks and work-area segments.** Managers attach operational notes, checklists and job/area segments within shifts. Employees see relevant instructions on mobile, confirm completed tasks and report a blocker with attachments; segment hours map to jobs for labor and payroll without double-counting the enclosing shift. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish a shift split between counter and kitchen with a closing checklist. Staff open it on mobile, complete tasks and report a blocker; managers inspect handover and reconcile segment totals to worked time.

- **LEAVE-001 — PTO balances and staffing-aware time off.** Employees view PTO/sick balances, request full/partial-day leave and see decisions; managers review coverage and policy limits before approving. Accruals, carryover and paid/unpaid codes are employer-configured with effective dates. Connected HR systems have one explicit leave authority under HR-001; approvals update availability and payroll classification without duplicate balances. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a US PTO policy, accrue a balance, request partial-day leave and approve with a coverage warning. Carry a year-end balance and reconcile paid/unpaid hours; connect HR authority and reject conflicting double decisions.

## Coverage employee experience and follow-up

- **COVER-001 — Employee shift swaps with final approval.** Employees offer a shift or propose an exchange; eligible coworkers consent and a scoped manager approves the exact exchange. Availability, qualification, rest, overtime, leave and coverage are rechecked at decision time. Pending swaps do not change responsibility; concurrent requests cannot assign the same shift twice. **Acceptance evidence:** On employee mobile accounts propose and accept a two-person swap, approve as manager and inspect both changed schedules/notices. Refuse an ineligible coworker, stale request and two competing approvals; retain the original assignee until completion.

- **COVER-002 — Open shifts offers and pickup.** Managers publish unassigned shifts to eligible staff or a controlled pool. Employees see job/site/rate details and request pickup; manager policy determines approved pickup versus explicit final approval. Atomic assignment closes all other offers, rechecks eligibility and prevents duplicate pickup or silent coverage loss. **Acceptance evidence:** Post an open shift, view it as eligible and ineligible staff and submit competing pickup requests. Approve one, verify all other offers close and current assignments/notices update exactly once.

- **COVER-003 — Call-out replacement and verified coverage.** Employees report an absence or managers record a call-out against a shift with minimal private reason disclosure. The manager sees a qualified available replacement shortlist, cost/overtime effects and timed offers; employees accept and a manager approves assignment. An unfilled shift escalates to the responsible manager and remains uncovered until accepted, then attendance is monitored through actual clock-in. **Acceptance evidence:** Call out before a shift, contact eligible replacements, let one offer expire, approve an accepted replacement and observe notice/acknowledgement and clock-in. A nonresponsive replacement reopens an actionable coverage exception without fabricating attendance.

- **NOTICE-001 — Delivered schedule notices and acknowledgement.** Publication, changes, pickups, swaps and cancellations create recipient-specific in-app, push, email and US SMS notices, using APNs/FCM, Resend and Twilio as supported delivery services. Deduplicated attempts expose queued, provider-accepted, delivered where observable, failed and employee-acknowledged states separately. Consent, quiet hours, urgent policy, US sender registration and opt-out preferences control contact; sensitive leave/pay data is excluded. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish and change a shift for consenting employees; receive notices through each configured real provider and acknowledge the exact revision. Inspect receipts, a provider rejection, opted-out/quiet-hour routing and duplicate callbacks; delivery alone cannot mark employee acknowledgement.

- **NOTICE-002 — Reminders escalation and payroll follow-up.** Managers set shift reminders, acknowledgement deadlines, open-offer expiry, late/no-show and missed-punch/break alerts, timesheet review cutoffs and payroll review reminders. Durable scheduled work survives restart, deduplicates per shift/revision/period, stops obsolete notices after resolution, respects employee contact settings and escalates unresolved exceptions to an accountable manager. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a shift and payroll period, receive a reminder, leave a schedule change unacknowledged, miss a punch and cross the review deadline. Restart the scheduler, resolve each item and inspect one appropriate escalation with no obsolete follow-up.

- **CHAT-001 — Team messaging and operational handover.** Staff and authorized managers use direct, group, site and shift threads, announcements and read acknowledgements, with attachments, search and retained handover context. Deactivated people lose access; private HR/payroll facts stay outside broad team threads. Shift-related conversations link to the actual schedule revision and cannot replace a formal swap or time approval. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a site announcement and shift handover, acknowledge and search them as staff, then transfer/deactivate a member and verify thread permissions. A chat request to swap still requires the COVER-001 approval journey.

- **CAL-001 — Google and Outlook calendar subscriptions.** Employees subscribe to private revocable iCalendar feeds in Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar. Published assignments, changes and cancellations use stable event identities and correct site time zones; private notes, coworkers and draft shifts are excluded. The app states provider-controlled refresh and does not present a subscription as immediate two-way scheduling. **Acceptance evidence:** Subscribe from Google and Outlook, publish/change/cancel an overnight shift and inspect refreshed events and timezone. Rotate/revoke the feed, confirm old access fails and verify another employee cannot retrieve it.

- **MOBILE-001 — Employee and manager mobile apps.** iOS and Android apps plus accessible responsive web cover schedules, availability, leave, swaps, pickups, call-outs, messaging, time capture and acknowledgements. Push deep-links to authorized current records; a revoked or shared device cannot expose another employee's work. Keyboard/screen-reader flows, clear pending/error states and recovery from weak connectivity cover core tasks. **Acceptance evidence:** Use realistic multi-site data at 1440px and 390px and on supported iOS/Android devices; complete the shift-change-to-clock journey, follow a push deep link, lose connectivity and sign out/revoke. Confirm accessible focus, current data and explicit unsent state.

## Attendance US policy and labor economics

- **CLOCK-001 — Shared kiosk clocks and device authority.** A manager enrolls and revokes a tablet/shared-kiosk time clock limited to approved sites. Employees identify themselves with individual PIN/session verification, clock jobs and breaks without viewing others' private records, and sign out between users. Kiosk administration requires separate authority; lost devices and repeated failed PINs can be locked. **Acceptance evidence:** Enroll a shared tablet, have two employees clock consecutive shifts and breaks, attempt identity guessing and cross-person reads, then revoke the tablet during a session. Inspect correctly attributed punches and denied further captures.

- **CLOCK-002 — Durable offline capture and reconnect reconciliation.** Phone and kiosk clients persist encrypted pending clock/break events across page closure, app/device restart and loss of service, showing captured versus received times and pending state. Reconnection reconciles stable identities exactly once, respects revoked authority and surfaces clock skew, out-of-order or conflicting captures for review without discarding original facts. **Acceptance evidence:** Clock and break while offline, close/restart the client, reconnect and retry. Read exactly one ordered event set and corrected worked time; simulate revoked access and conflicting capture, retaining an explicit unresolved record for a manager.

- **CLOCK-003 — Geofenced mobile clocking with privacy controls.** Employers configure per-site geofences and explicit mobile location permissions for punch-time verification, showing accuracy and reasoned exception paths when location is unavailable. Clocking location is limited to the event, with no background employee tracking; supervisors review disputed or out-of-zone punches without changing captured facts. Biometric or dedicated hardware verification is optional partner integration. **Acceptance evidence:** Clock inside/outside a site and with poor accuracy or location permission denied. Review a documented exception, inspect punch-time location and retention controls, then revoke device access; no off-shift location trail appears.

- **CLOCK-004 — Live attendance and late or no-show alerts.** Managers see who is scheduled, present, on break, late, missing or clocked at the wrong site, with freshness and unresolved conflicts. Scheduled shift boundaries and configured grace periods generate accountable late/no-show alerts and coverage actions; published or confirmed shifts alone never count as worked. **Acceptance evidence:** Run a service shift with an on-time, late, absent and offline employee. Compare the live attendance board to actual punches, receive a late/no-show escalation, arrange coverage and resolve only from observed or approved time facts.

- **BREAK-001 — Timed meals breaks and exceptions.** Employees start/end paid rest and unpaid meal breaks on phone or kiosk. Configured site/state policy checks timing, duration, missed/short breaks, waivers and attestations, producing reviewable exceptions and premium-pay inputs when applicable. Manual edits preserve employee facts and approval history; missing breaks cannot silently reduce payable work. **Acceptance evidence:** Clock a meal and paid rest break, miss or shorten a required meal and submit an attestation. Review the exception and a reasoned correction, then reconcile net/pay-code hours and any premium input in payroll preview.

- **TIME-003 — Missed punches employee review and exception resolution.** Employees review daily/period timesheets, submit missing-punch or correction requests and attest to work and breaks. Managers compare schedules with captured events, investigate long/open/overlapping time and approve exact reasoned changes with employee visibility. Exceptions stay assigned and open until resolved, and deadline reminders continue under NOTICE-002. **Acceptance evidence:** Miss clock-out, receive an exception, submit a correction as the employee, review/approve it as another authorized manager and inspect the original facts, attestation, reason and employee-visible outcome before payroll.

- **TIME-004 — Payroll period locks and controlled reopening.** Payroll administrators define pay periods, collect employee/manager signoff and lock approved hours. Separate reopening permission requires a reason and records who reopened which period; closed or provider-applied periods accept linked late/off-cycle adjustments instead of rewriting paid history. Concurrent edits and transfers recheck period state. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve and lock a period, deny an ordinary manager's edit/reopen, reopen with payroll authority and fresh signoff, then close after provider application. A later correction produces an explicit adjustment and cannot mutate the original paid period.

- **POLICY-001 — US overtime meal and rest policy rules.** Employers configure effective-dated FLSA workweeks and applicable state/local daily/weekly overtime, double time, consecutive-day, rest/meal, minor-work and minimum-rest rules. Planning warns before assignment/publication; actual hours aggregate across jobs/sites and classify regular/overtime/premiums with explainable rule/version inputs. Employer-reviewed policy and provider treatment of the regular rate remain explicit; changing rules never silently rewrites closed payroll. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure employer-reviewed federal and California scenarios, schedule cross-site hours that trigger daily/weekly rules and observe pre-publish warnings. Work/correct those hours and trace each pay classification to its effective policy; a new policy requires reviewed recalculation of open periods.

- **POLICY-002 — Predictive scheduling and fair-workweek changes.** Site-specific predictive-scheduling policies track advance notice, good-faith estimates, employee consent, schedule-change/cancellation premiums, rest between shifts and required records. A post-publication change previews consent/premium consequences and any employer-authorized exception; employees receive the changed revision and retain their response. Policy dependencies never silently waive an obligation. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a covered US location, publish a week and change/cancel a shift inside its notice window. Obtain required consent, show the premium and audit records, refuse an unapproved exception and carry approved premium inputs to payroll.

- **COST-001 — Scheduled versus actual labor cost and budgets.** Managers plan USD labor budgets by site/job/day/week and compare scheduled versus worked hours, wages, overtime, tips/premiums and configured employer burdens. Variance reports distinguish estimated, approved and provider-reconciled cost; rate-effective dates, cross-site hours and forecast sales are visible. Budget warnings precede publishing and overruns trigger accountable follow-up without hiding required paid time. **Acceptance evidence:** Build and work a multi-role week with rate changes, overtime and a call-out; inspect budget warnings and scheduled/actual cost by site. Correct time and reconcile the variance to approved hours and provider-applied payroll without counting an unworked shift.

- **TIPS-001 — Tips pooling and reviewed payroll allocation.** Managers import cash/card tip inputs from POS or employee declaration, configure employer-reviewed US pool eligibility, contribution/distribution and job/hour rules, and allocate by actual approved work. Staff review distributions and discrepancies; managers approve the exact pool revision. Excluded roles, rounding, corrections, tip-credit inputs and provider pay-code mapping remain traceable and never imply Lanyard moved money. **Acceptance evidence:** Import Square/Toast/Clover tip inputs, allocate a service pool across eligible worked roles, reject an excluded participant and reconcile totals/rounding. Approve a correction and transfer mapped tip lines to payroll with provider readback and employee-visible history.

- **REPORT-001 — Operational reports exports and scheduled delivery.** Authorized managers filter and export schedules, attendance/late/no-show, breaks/exceptions, PTO, worked hours, overtime, labor cost versus budget/sales, tip allocations and payroll reconciliation by site/job/person/period. Reports reconcile totals and source revisions, distinguish missing data, and support scheduled email delivery with role-scoped access and revocable download links. **Acceptance evidence:** Run a pay-period report over two locations, reconcile row totals to approved time/POS/payroll, export CSV and schedule a recipient-scoped digest. Verify a site manager cannot retrieve another site's wages and an unresolved offline punch remains visible as incomplete.

## Connected payroll sales HR and automation

- **PAY-001 — Payroll-ready classified hours and pay codes.** Payroll administrators map employee, employment type, job, location, PTO, regular/overtime/double-time, break premiums, scheduling premiums and tip codes before transfer. Approved hours and signed corrections carry effective policy/rate references and reconcile by period and employee. W-2 employees and supported 1099 contractor workflows are explicit; contractor exports cannot silently apply employee overtime/tax treatment. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a period with regular/overtime/PTO/tip lines and a late negative adjustment, resolve an unmapped pay code and review totals by W-2/1099 workflow. Export/transfer the exact approved revision and reconcile all classified minutes and amounts.

- **SYNC-001 — Approved transfer and provider-applied reconciliation.** A payroll administrator authorizes the exact reviewed provider submission. Durable idempotency keys and source/period fences prevent duplicate sends; transport queued/sent, provider accepted/rejected/partial, applied-to-payroll and paid states remain separate. Readback reconciles employee/pay-code totals, preserves unknown outcomes for investigation and supports reviewed corrections and off-cycle adjustments. Provider acceptance alone never means wages paid. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a supported real payroll account, submit approved hours, inspect acceptance and application readback, then induce partial rejection and a lost response. Resume without duplicate hours, resolve employee/pay-code discrepancies and carry a locked-period correction as a reviewed adjustment.

- **GUSTO-001 — Live Gusto payroll connection.** Connect a US Gusto company through approved production authorization, map employees/jobs/PTO/pay codes and transfer approved classified hours under SYNC-001. Provider permissions, supported employee/contractor paths and time-data versus payroll-application states are explicit; reauthorization and disconnect preserve source history without sending more work. **Acceptance evidence:** Authorize a real Gusto company, preview mappings and approved hours, submit with payroll-admin approval and reconcile provider-accepted/applied totals. Correct one employee, handle a revoked grant and demonstrate no duplicate submission after reconnect.

- **ADP-001 — Distinct ADP RUN and Workforce Now connectors.** US buyers connect ADP RUN or ADP Workforce Now using each product's supported production app/partner authorization. Separate capability matrices map employees, jobs, regular/overtime/PTO/tips and periods; approved transfers and readback follow SYNC-001. A connection to one product is never represented as support for the other. **Acceptance evidence:** For each named ADP product authorize a supported company, review employee/pay-code mapping, submit a period and reconcile accepted/applied totals including one rejection and later correction. Verify disconnect stops future sends and preserves the exact submission history.

- **PAYCHEX-001 — Live Paychex payroll connection.** Connect the buyer's supported US Paychex payroll product through production app/partner access, map people and earning codes, and send approved period hours, tips and adjustments with readback under SYNC-001. A formatted file is labeled export-only and cannot satisfy this live integration promise; required provider agreements are delivery work. **Acceptance evidence:** Authorize a real supported Paychex account, map and send reviewed hours, receive and reconcile accepted/applied totals, then resolve a rejected tip/pay code and a closed-period adjustment without replaying paid hours.

- **QB-001 — QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Payroll mapping.** Connect supported US QuickBooks Online/QuickBooks Payroll company configurations, explicitly stating which plan, time API and payroll edition each path supports. Reconcile employee/job/class/pay-code mappings and authorized time transfer to provider application. Online accounting connectivity alone is not evidence of QuickBooks Payroll support; unsupported API fields require a named reviewed provider/partner path. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a declared supported Online/Payroll configuration, map jobs and regular/overtime/PTO/tip codes, transfer a reviewed period and reconcile provider totals. Demonstrate a permission/edition mismatch and an approved correction without claiming unsupported payroll application.

- **POS-001 — Square sales staff and tip inputs.** Authorize Square POS accounts/locations and reconcile sales, refunds, staff/job identifiers and available tip inputs into location-local demand and labor-to-sales reports. Incremental cursors, deduplication, backfill and freshness labels prevent duplicate or stale sales. Payment capture/refunds remain in Square; importing sales cannot approve hours or tip pools. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect two Square locations, backfill and incrementally sync sales/refunds/tips, map staff and compare labor-to-net-sales. Replay events and disconnect one location; totals remain stable and that location is visibly stale.

- **POS-002 — Toast restaurant sales and labor connection.** Connect supported Toast restaurants through Toast production/Partner Connect permissions and required connector subscriptions or fees. Reconcile per-location sales, refunds, jobs, available tips and time inputs with clear source ownership; duplicate Toast clock data cannot double-count Lanyard punches. Demand and cost reports retain freshness and source provenance. **Acceptance evidence:** Complete actual Toast access for two restaurants, map jobs and source clocks, backfill sales/tips and compare a service's labor report. Deny an unapproved restaurant and replay a source event without duplicating sales or time.

- **POS-003 — Clover sales terminals and employee mapping.** Connect Clover merchant/location data for sales/refunds, employee identifiers, available clock/tip inputs and labor-to-sales analysis, with an explicit authority choice when Clover terminals supply punches. Approvals and corrections stay in Lanyard's time workflow; POS event retries cannot create duplicate work. **Acceptance evidence:** Authorize a Clover merchant, map employees and terminal punches, sync sales/tips and reconcile a shift. Retry events, change an employee mapping and inspect rejected conflicts and freshness before payroll approval.

- **POS-004 — Shopify POS and Lightspeed sales adapters.** Buyers connect Shopify POS and supported Lightspeed Restaurant K series locations for staff/sales inputs used in demand and labor reporting. - **HR-001 — Willow and BambooHR roster and leave authority.** Connect BambooHR and the sibling [Willow](https://github.com/just-every/run-willow) for employee lifecycle, employment attributes and leave. ## Push further

Agents serve the same staffing-to-payroll job. Each delegated action is source-backed, revocable and bounded by the named human decisions; an uncertain or unresolved outcome remains open.

- **COVERAGE-001 — Carry an absence or demand swing to verified coverage.** With manager-authorized contact and staffing policies, the coverage agent reads the affected shift, demand delta, qualifications, availability, leave, cross-site actual/approved hours, fatigue/rest limits, rates and prior responses. It proposes qualified available non-overtime replacements first, explains exceptions, sends authorized offers, collects employee consent, expires losing offers and brings the exact change to a human manager. It carries the approved change through schedule notices and payroll preparation, then checks acknowledgement and actual clock-in; only evidence closes coverage. Revocation stops further outreach and stale data triggers fresh review. **Acceptance evidence:** Report a call-out and demand increase across two sites; inspect ranked source-backed options and policy refusals, collect replies, approve one exact diff and observe closed offers, delivered/acknowledged changes and payroll draft. Miss the replacement's clock-in and require reopened coverage escalation; revoke agent authority and prove outreach stops.

- **TRADEOFF-001 — Explain cost fairness and service alternatives.** The planning agent compares candidate schedules using POS demand, service requirements, effective wage/policy data, employee preferences and equitable distribution of hours/undesirable shifts. It shows USD cost, overtime, missed preferences, coverage/service risk and uncertainty for each alternative with source facts. Managers choose objectives and approve exceptions; protected traits are excluded from ranking and the agent cannot waive required qualifications or breaks. **Acceptance evidence:** Ask why a cheaper roster gives worse coverage or unfair closing shifts; inspect alternatives, calculation inputs and missing data, change a fairness/service objective and review the resulting diff. No proposal publishes without human decision and an impossible constraint stays visible.

- **CUTOFF-001 — Resolve payroll exceptions before the cutoff.** The payroll agent monitors actual punches, breaks, approved revisions, mapping failures and provider acceptance/application against the pay-period cutoff. It explains discrepancies in plain language, requests factual corrections from employees and follows up with accountable managers until resolved. Workers confirm facts, managers approve exact time and payroll administrators alone authorize submission/off-cycle treatment; agent reasoning cannot invent hours, hide an exception or call accepted time paid. **Acceptance evidence:** Approach payroll cutoff with a missed punch, break exception, unmapped employee and partial provider rejection. Follow agent requests through employee confirmation, manager approval and administrator submission, inspect provider readback and remaining unknowns, then handle a later correction without rewriting paid history.

- **LEARN-001 — Learn from forecast errors and unresolved outcomes.** The agent compares forecast demand, published staffing, actual attendance, sales/service outcomes and unresolved exceptions, attributing known error sources and retaining uncertainty. It proposes revised demand assumptions, offer timing and objective weights with a measured comparison and versioned explanation. Managers approve model/policy changes; protected traits, unobserved work and unresolved payroll are never imputed as success, and reverting a model never alters historical records. **Acceptance evidence:** After several comparable service periods, inspect forecast-versus-actual errors including a no-show and stale POS interval, review a proposed assumption change and its holdout comparison, approve or decline it and revert if needed. Unobserved outcomes remain unknown in learning and reporting.
